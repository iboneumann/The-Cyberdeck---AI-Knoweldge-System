🧠 RAG: Internal Knowledge vs. External Reference

Data to be Analyzed (Standard Parsing): In a more conventional use case, RAG is used to

resource being analyzed for a specific task, not a persistent part of the model's identity.

💡 Why Use RAG for Corporate Knowledge?
Applying the "integrated knowledge" model to corporate data is a compelling strategy for
several reasons:
    Cost-Effectiveness: It's far more economical than retraining or fine-tuning a
    foundational model, which is computationally expensive and resource-intensive.
    RAG allows you to gain domain-specific performance without these costs.

Access to Current & Proprietary Information: It grounds the AI in your company's live
data—proprietary knowledge, internal wikis, customer data, and latest research—preventing
it from relying on outdated or generic information from its initial training.

Enhanced Trust and Control: The model can provide citations to its source documents
(e.g., internal company policies or data reports), allowing employees to verify the
information. Developers also have greater control, as they can easily update the knowledge
base or restrict access to sensitive information without altering the core model.

Reduced Hallucinations: By anchoring the model's responses in your authoritative internal
data, RAG significantly lowers the risk of the AI inventing facts or "hallucinating"
incorrect information, which is crucial for business decisions.
